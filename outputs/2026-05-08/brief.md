This 2-minute Mango Ragi Overnight Jar is your new summer breakfast obsession.

🫙 What you need:
• 2 tbsp PantryPulse Ragi Flour
• 1 tbsp PantryPulse Organic Jaggery Powder
• ½ cup curd
• Fresh Alphonso mango chunks
• A pinch of cardamom

Mix. Layer. Refrigerate overnight. Wake up happy.
